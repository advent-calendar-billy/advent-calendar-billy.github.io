title: For which tasks would you NOT use AI?
date: 2026-09-15
---

![Panel discussion at Wayfair](/media/wayfair-panel.jpeg)

A few days ago I [shared the stage](https://www.linkedin.com/events/7493371676617822208/) with Wayfair's senior leadership for a panel discussion. One of the questions I was asked was "For which tasks would you NOT use AI?"

1. I don’t use AI for things which I find joy in doing myself. I do a fair bit of writing and theater improv, and I would never use AI to write sketches, for example.
2. I also avoid using AI when communicating with other people. If someone asks me something, it’s probably because they want to hear my opinion. I will sometimes use AI to help answer the question – especially if my setup has access to context and tooling that the other person lacks – and I might include a quote from my AI, but I will always provide my own commentary and recommendations.
